# Demo Video Script — SyncCRM AI (60 seconds)

**Setup:** open the public demo page (`/`), start screen recording. Make sure
“Reset demo data” was clicked recently so the seeded duplicates are present.
Speak naturally — timings are approximate.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook**

> "Small businesses don't have a leads problem — they have a messy CRM problem.
> Duplicate contacts, nobody knows who's hot, and follow-up emails never get
> written. This AI layer fixes all three."

*Action: hero + dashboard with the contacts table visible.*

**[0:08–0:20] AI scoring**

> "Every contact gets an AI score — and it shows its work. Sarah Mitchell:
> 91 out of 100, Tier A. Business email, owner title, active yesterday — every
> point is explained. No black box."

*Action: click Sarah Mitchell in the table; scroll the “Why this AI score”
panel.*

**[0:20–0:32] Auto-drafted follow-up**

> "Then it drafts the follow-up for you — in three tones — using her actual
> signals. I can regenerate it, queue it for review… but it never sends on its
> own."

*Action: show the email draft card; switch Friendly → Professional; click
“Regenerate draft”, then “Queue send (simulated)”.*

**[0:32–0:45] Duplicate detection**

> "Watch this — the CRM found two Sarah Mitchells from different imports.
> Same email, same company. One click merges them, combines the engagement
> history, and re-scores."

*Action: scroll to the duplicates panel; click “Merge records →”; point at
the merge event in the feed and the “+1 merged” tag.*

**[0:45–0:55] Live sync feed + add contact**

> "And everything streams into this live sync feed — the same feed that in
> production keeps the CRM, inbox and calendar in sync. Add a contact and the
> AI scores it instantly."

*Action: point at live feed events arriving; click “Add a contact”, fill
quickly, submit; point at the new scored contact.*

**[0:55–1:00] Close**

> "This is a concept demo — but the real version is the same scoring rules
> running server-side, wired to your actual CRM. If your contact list is a
> mess, let's talk."

---

**Recording tips**

- Click “Reset demo data” right before recording for a clean take (restores
  the two seeded duplicate groups).
- Keep the browser at ~1440px wide so the table and detail panel sit side by
  side.
- The live feed adds an event every 4 seconds — mention it while it happens;
  pause it if it distracts from narration.
- For a second 15-second clip: merge the Tom Becker duplicate to show the
  score change after merging.
